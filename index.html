QU4RTET — the leading open-source EPCIS platform

QU4RTET is a powerful, extensible open-source platform for serialization and supply-chain traceability, built on GS1 standards. Gracenote Systems is the premier partner for taking it from repository to production.

  • Full EPCIS 1.2 & CBV support for compliant event capture and exchange
  • Extensible rule engine for validation, transformation, and routing
  • Advanced serial-number pools and allocation management
  • Open standards, open source — enterprise traceability without vendor lock-in

Chorus UI — the enterprise interface for QU4RTET

Chorus UI is the modern, production-ready interface that makes QU4RTET operable for real teams. Designed for regulated industries, it gives operators an intuitive, role-based experience for master data, capture rules, and end-to-end operations — with full auditability and control. Chorus UI is a Gracenote Systems enterprise product, offered only by us.

  • Master data, FDA-sourced product import, and number range management
  • Warewolf warehouse module — barcode-driven receiving, picking, packing & shipping
  • Real-time inventory with lot, expiry, and hierarchical locations
  • Complete audit trail and automatic, compliant EPCIS event generation
  • Fully responsive — runs cleanly on phone and tablet, right from the warehouse floor
