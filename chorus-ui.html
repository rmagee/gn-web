Master Material Management

One source of truth for products, companies & locations

Every trade item, company, and location your serialization program depends on lives in a single, structured record — not spreadsheets, not tribal knowledge. Chorus UI gives operators a clean interface to manage the master data that QU4RTET's rule engine and EPCIS capture depend on to run correctly.

  • Trade items with GTIN-14, NDC, dosage form, strength, and pack data in one record
  • Companies and trading partners with GS1 company prefixes, centrally managed
  • Locations mapped to GLN13 / SGLN identifiers, ready for EPCIS events
  • Product imagery attached to every item, for fast visual identification on the floor

Direct From openFDA

Pull regulated product data straight from the FDA

Manually keying in NDC numbers, dosage forms, and labeler data is slow and error-prone — and in a regulated industry, errors are expensive. Chorus UI's FDA import tool queries the openFDA Drug NDC database live, so your master data starts accurate.

  • Live search against the openFDA Drug NDC database by brand, generic name, manufacturer, or NDC
  • Select and import matching products straight into Trade Items
  • Assign imported items to the right company in the same step
  • Cuts transcription errors out of regulated product data entry

Serialization At Scale

Allocate, monitor, and safely test serial number pools

Running out of serial numbers mid-shift stops a production line. Chorus UI gives you visibility into every number pool — how it's configured, how fast it's depleting, and a safe way to test it before it's trusted with real inventory.

  • Dedicated pools per product line or trading partner, each with its own API key
  • Configurable thresholds so you know exactly when a pool needs refilling
  • Randomized, sequential, or list-based numbering regions, mixed as needed
  • A built-in test-allocation tool to validate a pool before it goes live
Talk through your numbering strategy

Full chain-of-custody, searchable in seconds

Under the hood, Chorus UI is a front end on QU4RTET's rule engine and EPCIS core — surfaced as something an operator can actually read. Every capture rule, every event, and every entry's full hierarchy is a click away, with the auditability regulated supply chains require.

  • Configurable rule engine for EPCIS capture, filtering, and output — inspectable step by step
  • Every entry's full event history, parent/child hierarchy, and disposition in one view

  • Complete, exportable audit trail for DSCSA and GxP-aligned deployments
